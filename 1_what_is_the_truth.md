### Definitions
Person X: for the sake of articulation, let's represent it by a person; it could be a friend of yours.
Object: Something of never-changing properties, unbounded by interpretation, a source of truth that can be directly observed.
Concept: A layer of abstraction of borrowed properties that isn't necessarily true or representative of the object/reality

### An elaboration on concepts

Imagine your person X, you might even say he/she is an object. But I beg to differ that he/she is a concept.
A concept because your interpretation of him/her is different from someone else's. Person X in other words, only exists in your mind and your mind only.
Likewise, any object you can think of, exists in your mind as a concept... Even you. It cannot be an object because it isn't truthful as an object. 

#### Advanced thought about concepts
What we perceive through our five senses, what we perceive about how the world works, what we perceive through memory, or what we perceive as beliefs are all projections in our mind. 
And those projections are all concepts, and it is not possible to deduce them as truth as they are internal. 

### Why should we even bother?
I asked a friend this:
> "The odds of existing are infinitesimally small, nearly zero. What can we do to honour our existence?"
I was surprised at how honest and aware his answer was:
> "To make the most out of life"
It was honest because most people would give an actual exercise for existence (do X,Y,Z). 
It was far from an actual answer because of the dilemma of the word "most". It is an unreachable goal because there is always "more x,y,z" that you can do, and falling short means not living as the answer points to.

How can we honor existence if we never exist (in reality). We all exist in a dream-state; we exist in our own mind as a concept borrowed from externally reinforced attributes and beliefs. 
If you tell someone he is X and everyone agrees that he is X, he will believe it himself, regardless if it's true or not. Beliefs are not self-defined by borrowed concepts and ideas

### Then how can we find truth
If truth cannot be known externally, the only other option is to look internally. Because we cannot strip external concepts until they are bare-bones objects. 
I.e we cannot know X as X knows itself. Cliché, but to know the truth you have to "know thyself". 
We could challenge all our conceptual properties and attributes that we think is representative of us "as objects", until only an "object" exists because that should be the truth.
